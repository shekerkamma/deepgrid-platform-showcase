## Slide 54 · A100 two-channel · How it fits

A100 two-channel · How it fits. How it works Sense, compute on SoC2, decide, act — the same four stages in every product. DEEPGRID SEMI · PRODUCT LINES · MANAGEMENT PROJECTIONS 54 SIGNAL CHAIN 1 Surround sensing reads containers, cranes, lanes and people in the yard 2 SoC2 localises against the yard map and tracks moving obstacles 3 Planner sequences the pick, the move and the drop against the yard schedule 4 The vehicle executes and reports back to the terminal system What the silicon does here Under a quay crane, satellite positioning degrades exactly where precision matters most. Dynamic fusion on the SoC2 holds 7 cm in the open and 22 cm under the crane, where RTK-GNSS alone falls away — and that positioning is what makes crane-synchronised dispatch possible at all. What this product depends on Terminal integration work and berth-level commissioning, which is slow and bespoke. Twenty-eight point eight crore. A step on a ladder rather than a destination — but the ladder is what lets one die address several price points at once.
