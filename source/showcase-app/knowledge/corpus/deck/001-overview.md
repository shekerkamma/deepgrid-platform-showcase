## Slide 3 · Why now

Why now. The mandate is a demand floor, not a forecast Compliance dates are set. The buying decision is when, not whether. DEEPGRID SEMI · PRODUCT LINES · MANAGEMENT PROJECTIONS 03 1.0 M TRUCKS A YEAR IN SCOPE 0.5 M NEW BUILD, ANNUALLY 0.5 M EXISTING FLEET RETROFIT 18,000 OUR FY2032 AD2 UNITS MAR 2025 Rule notified Sets the ADAS obligation for N2/N3 vehicles APR 2026 New models Drowsiness, blind-spot and lane-departure required OCT 2026 Existing models Obligation reaches the in-service fleet OCT 2027 Braking Advanced emergency braking on a separate schedule What a floor means for an investor A mandated purchase removes the hardest question in hardware — whether the buyer will pay for safety. Execution risk stays ours: certification and installation throughput. The demand is legislated, not forecast. Driver-drowsiness, blind-spot and lane-departure obligations land on new and existing commercial vehicles on fixed dates. About a million trucks a year fall in scope. For a fleet operator the question is not whether to buy — it is whose chip is inside when they do.

## Slide 5 · The silicon

The silicon. What the part actually is One 28 nm die under every SKU in this deck. Configuration changes; the compute does not. DEEPGRID SEMI · PRODUCT LINES · MANAGEMENT PROJECTIONS 05 PARAMETER SPECIFICATION WHY AN INVESTOR SHOULD CARE Process TSMC 28 nm · single die, ~57 mm² Monolithic, not chiplet — less cost and less risk. Compute 32,768 MACs at 600 MHz · 39.3 TOPS 64 compute cubes of 512 MACs. Derived, not rounded up. Memory 102.4 GB/s dual-channel LPDDR5 Bandwidth, not TOPS, binds multi-camera fusion. Transformer Hardware softmax · weight-stationary attention The operation an FPGA cannot do at frame rate. Safety Lockstep cores plus a hardware root of trust The path to an automotive safety rating. Throughput Eleven sensor channels fused in 8.6 ms Of a 33.3 ms frame budget — 74% left unused. The one number that explains the portfolio A part that clears eleven channels in a quarter of its frame budget configures downward into a one-channel module, a mirror unit, a radar pod or a forklift kit without redesigning the compute. That headroom is why fifteen SKUs are one tapeout, not fifteen programmes. Here is the part itself. Thirty-two thousand seven hundred and sixty-eight multiply-accumulate units at six hundred megahertz, a hundred and two gigabytes a second of memory bandwidth, hardware softmax, and lockstep cores for the automotive safety path. The number that matters most: it fuses eleven sensor channels in eight point six milliseconds of a thirty-three millisecond frame. Seventy-four percent of the frame is left unused, and that headroom is why one die becomes fifteen products.
