## Slide 19 · AD2 · How it works

AD2 · How it works. How it works Sense, compute on SoC2, decide, act — the same four stages in every product. DEEPGRID SEMI · PRODUCT LINES · MANAGEMENT PROJECTIONS 19 SIGNAL CHAIN 1 Camera and radar watch the forward path and the blind quarters 2 SoC2 fuses both into one object list with range and closing speed 3 Collision, lane and blind-spot logic decide whether to warn 4 Driver is warned, and the brake interface is signalled where fitted What the silicon does here The box takes eleven channels at once — seven 8MP RGB cameras on GMSL2, two thermal and front/rear 4D 77 GHz radar — and fuses the whole suite in 8.6 ms of a 33.3 ms frame. That leaves 74% of the frame budget unused, which is the margin that lets one part serve every sensor configuration below it. The driver is warned, and where the vehicle supports it, the brake interface is signalled. Radar matters here because closing speed is what a braking decision actually turns on.
