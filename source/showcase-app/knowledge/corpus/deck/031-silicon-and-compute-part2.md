DEEPGRID SEMI · PRODUCT LINES · MANAGEMENT PROJECTIONS 45 SIGNAL CHAIN 1 The customer runs their own hardware and their own sensors 2 Our models do the perception work inside their runtime 3 Their system decides and acts 4 They ship under their own brand, with our perception inside What the silicon does here The licence sells the model and toolchain without the board. It is the only line that carries no bill of materials at all, which is why it prices on value rather than on cost. What this product depends on A defensible model advantage and a signed licence; neither is evidenced yet. And here is the failure mode, with the workload held constant. Spread evenly, fifty-six requests give seven per bank. Collide them all onto bank zero and seven banks sit idle while the array stalls. Spread the addresses again and the capacity returns. What decides which of those you get is tensor layout, address hashing, arbitration and compiler scheduling — and the request counts here are a controlled illustration, not measured utilisation.
