## Systematic Methodology for Identifying DeepGrid’s ICP (ICP and GTM Strategy (Jul 2026) · Systematic Methodology for Identifying DeepGrid’s ICP)

A common point of failure for fabless semiconductor startups is targeting broad, unconstrained market segments without evaluating customer buying behaviors, technical integration friction, and regulatory timelines1. To optimize its limited Pre-Series A capital, DeepGrid must utilize a rigorous, multi-dimensional diagnostic framework to identify, score, and prioritize potential customer segments1. The systematic methodology relies on five critical strategic evaluation dimensions: Regulatory Compulsion Index: This dimension measures whether the customer's purchase is discretionary or driven by immediate, non-discretionary legislative mandates1. Segments facing hard regulatory compliance deadlines (such as India's upcoming heavy vehicle safety standards or Saudi Arabia's tracking laws) are prioritized because they exhibit condensed sales cycles and low marketing friction4. Technical Integration & Certification Friction: This parameter evaluates the engineering hours, architectural modifications, and safety homologation testing (such as ISO 26262 functional safety, AIS-004 electromagnetic compatibility, and AIS-189 cybersecurity certifications) required to design DeepGrid's silicon into the customer's platform7. Higher integration complexity delays time-to-revenue and increases cash burn1.
