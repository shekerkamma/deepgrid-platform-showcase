The market structure this plan has to survive An adversarial review of this document found that the buyer described above is real but describes a minority of the Indian market. The structure of Indian trucking is the single most important fact about our distribution problem, and it was previously absent from this plan: MARKET-STRUCTURE FACT VALUE Trucks on Indian roads 12.5M SOURCED Truck operators 3.5M SOURCED Operators owning fewer than five trucks 75–80% SOURCED Share of India’s freight moved by sub-five-truck operators 85% SOURCED Implied kits per month to hit the FY32 plan 4,000 EVIDENCE arithmetic on §8 ASSUMPTION What this does to the buyer definition above. “One signer, no committee” is accurate for an organised fleet and misleading as a description of the market. Most Indian trucks belong to operators with fewer than five vehicles, which means the 5M+ mandated base is not a base we can sell into directly at any realistic cost of acquisition. Two consequences this plan now adopts: our addressable segment is the organised market, defined below; and everything beyond it must be reached through a channel (§6), not a sales force.
