THE CHIP ECONOMICS Cost Clarity — Three Numbers VCs Always Confuse NRE (one-time design cost) vs per-chip cost vs board BOM — these are three completely different numbers. ~$3M <$4 → NRE Cost Die Cost ~$30 → Board BOM (one-time, paid once) (at 1M chips) (what we sell) 28nm tape-out: Muse/GSMC MPW. Our total upfront design cost. Nvidia spends $100M+ on NRE — we spend $2M because our architecture is purpose-built, not general-purpose. At mass production of 1 million chips, our die cost is <$4. From Muse/GSMC quotation — verifiable. TSMC 28nm at 95% yield, mature node. Our fundamental cost advantage. Die ($3) + carrier board + connectors + packaging = ~$30 full board BOM. At ₹2.5L ADAS kit price, gross margin reaches 84% at ASIC scale. At 100 units (FPGA, 2026): cost $600, sell $800 → 38% GM · At 10,000 units (ASIC, 2027+): cost $30, sell $200 → 84% GM
