## Three things this changes. EBITDA breakeven is FY2028 at +₹2.59 Cr, and net income turns … (BP1A India Autonomous Trucking Plan · p. 29)

Three things this changes. EBITDA breakeven is FY2028 at +₹2.59 Cr, and net income turns positive in FY2029 at +₹19.85 Cr — the plan reaches profitability one year after the raise, not at some unstated future point. Gross margin runs 53.4% → 88.4%, so the 88% figure is the model’s planned blended margin rather than an aspiration, though it remains a blend and not a kit-level number (§5). And FY32 EBITDA margin is 38.7%, not the 43% quoted in the June 2026 Information Memorandum — the lower figure is the one to use. The assumption the model rests on, unchanged from the earlier warning. Every ASP in the Revenue Master is held flat from FY27 to FY32 — the truck kit at ₹2.50L, the smart mirror at ₹0.50L, the dies at their launch prices. Indian mandate-created hardware markets have historically eroded ASP faster than COGS (§7). A Series-A model needs an erosion case: at 8–12% annual ASP decline from FY29 ASSUMPTION the FY32 ADAS line would fall materially below ₹914 Cr even with volumes held. That sensitivity is the single most valuable thing to add to v2.2 before this goes to an institutional investor.
