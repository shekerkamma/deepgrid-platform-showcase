## Shravan, You have offered to come in as an investor, and separately you have said you … (BP1B USA Proposal · p. 2)

Shravan, You have offered to come in as an investor, and separately you have said you would like to come in as a co-founder and bring your experience taking businesses international. I want to take the second offer more seriously than the first, because I think it is worth more to this company than the cheque — and because it deserves a real answer rather than an enthusiastic one. So I have written you a document about America specifically, kept apart from our India plan. I did that deliberately. Our India story is genuinely strong, and if I had merged the two you would have found the Indian tailwind quietly carrying the American argument. You would have discovered that yourself in month four, and you would have been right to be annoyed about it. What follows is what I actually believe, including the parts that make our US position look smaller than a founder normally admits in writing. Where a number is from our own material I have said so; where it is from outside research I have cited it. Everything I have not verified is marked as an assumption. I would rather you enter this knowing exactly what is unproven than have you find out after you have put your name on it. There is one thing I want to say plainly at the start. I take delivery — the silicon, the certification, the engineering. I am proposing that you take the market, starting with America.
