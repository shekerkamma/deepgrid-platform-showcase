That is one of three business models global autonomous trucking has already proven out, and the other two both involve owning or operating the vehicle: EXTERNAL REFERENCE Aurora Innovation licenses the "Aurora Driver" compute-and-software stack to OEMs and carriers per mile — asset-light, closest to DeepGrid's current model; Gatik owns or leases and directly operates a fleet of box trucks running fixed, repeatable middlemile retail routes — asset-heavy, selling delivery capacity, not a component; Kodiak Robotics blends highway driver-out technology licensing with government/off-road contracts and some owned-fleet operation — a hybrid.
