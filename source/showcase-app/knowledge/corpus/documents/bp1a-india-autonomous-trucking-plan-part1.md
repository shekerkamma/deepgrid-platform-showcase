## Executive summary. DeepGrid Semi sells the certification stamp, not the autonomy dream. … (BP1A India Autonomous Trucking Plan · p. 4)

Executive summary. DeepGrid Semi sells the certification stamp, not the autonomy dream. MoRTH GSR 184(E), live April 2026, mandates certified ADAS on every new N2/N3 commercial truck sold in India — a regulatory demand floor across a 5M+ truck base. The AD2 kit prices at ₹2.3L (₹2–2.5L band) PLAN against imports at ₹6.5L (Mobileye EyeQ6) to ₹15L (Nvidia Orin) — a ~2.8–6.5× gap ASSUMPTION ARITHMETIC PLAN . Owning the compute (28nm die, packaged part ₹1,750 ) lets us hold that price while gross margin expands from 45% (FPGA phase, FY27) to ~72% in the ASIC phase. Three stacked motions run on one hardware platform: (1) fleet retrofit kits now — no regulatory gate, revenue from day one; (2) OEM/Tier-1 line-fit for the mandate channel — the volume engine, entered after AIS-162/188 certification; (3) privateland driverless at ports, mines and plant yards — sold as per-vehicle-per-month software at 90%+ gross margin. The first retrofit customers are plant operators with private yards: one logo carries the kit sale, the data flywheel, and the future driverless contract. Primary planning numbers, from the working model (Business Plan v2.2): ADAS segment revenue ₹914 Cr at FY32 inside a company total of ₹1,388 Cr, with EBITDA breakeven in FY2028. Traction today: ₹1.01 Cr defence revenue CONTRACTED MEASURED ; AD0-class perception proven on FPGA (40 fps YOLOv11n ).
