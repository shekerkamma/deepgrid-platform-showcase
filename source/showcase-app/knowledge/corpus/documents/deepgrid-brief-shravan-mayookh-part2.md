W HY F LIG HT CONT ROL + V IO + AI MUS T LIV E ON ONE CHIP FC — Fly VIO — Know Where You Are AI — See & Decide The flight-control loop keeps the drone in the air — real-time, deterministic, every millisecond. It cannot wait on another chip. GPS is jammed along the LoC/LAC and never works indoors. Visual-inertial navigation is the only positioning that survives — geometry, not GPS. Onboard detection and obstacle avoidance when the comms link is gone — the drone must think for itself, on the drone. Fused on one die, they become a hardware fail-safe: when the link or GPS fails, the FC loop reads VIO position directly — same silicon, same clock, no bus, no second board — and flies itself home. Split across chips, that path gains latency, weight, power and failure points; a drone falling over a city or into stacked inventory is a lawsuit.
