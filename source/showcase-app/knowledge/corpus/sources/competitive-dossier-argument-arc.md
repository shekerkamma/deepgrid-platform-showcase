# DeepGrid Semi — sources/competitive-dossier-argument-arc

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## Act I The verdict

What should DeepGrid do, before any of the evidence? The answer, the gates that fund it, and what live research changed.

## Act II The field

Who occupies India CV ADAS, what is the actual rule, and what does each arena reward? The notified instrument, the scale of the field, and the line that divides it.

## Act III The contest

What is each rival TO DeepGrid — substitute, gatekeeper, channel, different job, narrative or supplier — and how much of this deck should that relationship buy? The one contest to win, the three accounts to sell to, the three markets to leave, the two claims to counter, and the supplier layer that sets the cost floor.

## Act IV The ranking

Who actually blocks us, in what order, and what do they do when we start winning? The re-derived ranking, the obstruction axis it was hiding, and the contests to lose deliberately.

## Act V The wedge

What exactly does DeepGrid sell, at what price, and what should it build versus buy? The bounded offer, what cannot be said about price, and the make-or-buy line.

## Act VI The plan

What must be true in 90 days, and who owns each gate? Owners, triggers and the gates that decide whether the position is real.

## Appendix The rest of the field

For the six companies Act III no longer leads with, what was the evidence? Every rating in the main line is still argued somewhere. Re-weighting moved these pages out of the argument; it did not withdraw them.
