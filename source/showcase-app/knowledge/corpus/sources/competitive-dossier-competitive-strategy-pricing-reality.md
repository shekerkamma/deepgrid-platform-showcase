## No competitor's India unit price is quotable — say so before you are asked

Every cost-advantage slide invites a question the evidence cannot answer. Two rows of the ledger establish why, and the answer is market structure rather than a search failure. If a slide carries a cost comparison, it must name the two things being compared and confirm they are the same scope. The retracted 774× and 12.9× claims both failed exactly this test.
