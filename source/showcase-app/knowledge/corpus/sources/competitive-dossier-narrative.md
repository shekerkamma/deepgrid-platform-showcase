# DeepGrid Semi — sources/competitive-dossier-narrative

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## The core message

DeepGrid cannot win India CV ADAS as a full-system supplier — ZF and Aptiv own braking, ECU, calibration, approval and warranty. It can win as the bounded perception layer an accountable integrator embeds, and the only thing between here and there is reproducible evidence that four funded gates buy in 90 days.
