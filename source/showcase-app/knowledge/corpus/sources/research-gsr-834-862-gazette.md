## The preamble is decisive on G.S.R. 184(E)

The notified rule states it was made "following consideration of public objections and suggestions to draft rules published as G.S.R 184(E) dated 20th March, 2025, under section 212(1) of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988." G.S.R. 184(E) is therefore named, by the operative instrument itself, as the superseded draft. It is not the law and cannot be cited as the compliance hook.

## What G.S.R. 862(E) changed

It substituted rule 96(12) to read "Vehicle Stability Function as per IS 11852:2019 and Advanced Emergency Braking Systems, as per AIS-162". The operative AEBS provision is 862(E)'s text, not 834(E)'s. Any citation of 834(E) alone for AEBS is incomplete. Also substituted rule 93(1)(B)(i) so ADAS sensors and casings (BSIS, MOIS) are excluded from overall-width measurement.

## Precision note on secondary reporting

Fortune India (13 Feb 2026) reports IS 11852:2019 and "ESC under AIS 162" as mandatory "from October 1, 2027", dropping the 1 Jan 2027 new-model stage and naming ESC where the instrument says Vehicle Stability Function. Secondary trade press garbles this timetable in both directions — use the gazette text.
