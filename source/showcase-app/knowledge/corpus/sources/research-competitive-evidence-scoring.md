# DeepGrid Semi — sources/research-competitive-evidence-scoring

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## Decision: no composite competitor score is issued

The contract fails a run where "scores or charts use undefined metrics" and where "source coverage is uneven but confidence is hidden". Both conditions would apply to a ranked competitor score here: - 4 of 7 cohort members have unit price = UNVERIFIED (EV-20 … EV-23) - 0 of 7 hold Indian ADAS certification (EV-08 and the deck's own table) - the two strongest India-present competitors found (bitsensing, ZF WABCO) publish no unit price at all (EV-16) A weighted score across those inputs would rank source availability, not competitive position, and would then be charted as if it were the latter. That is precisely the error the ledger exists to prevent.

## What is scored instead: claim status

Each claim currently in the deck is scored on whether evidence supports it. This is a scoring model over claims, not over companies. Status Definition Deck consequence VERIFIED Primary or corroborated current source supports the claim as stated Keep; upgrade the evidence tag REFRAMED Claim is directionally right but the metric or comparison basis is wrong Restate on the correct basis REFUTED Evidence contradicts the claim as stated Remove or correct UNVERIFIED No public source located; claim rests on the company only Downgrade to "company claim", keep the label visible RESOLVED Deck flagged an open item that evidence now closes Remove the open item

## Confidence in this run

Medium-high on regulation, medium on substitutes, none on import prices. The regulatory findings are primary-sourced from gazette text and are the most reliable output of this run. The substitute-band findings rest on vendor buyer-guides, which are credible but commercially motivated — they are marked secondary / medium and should not be presented as independent benchmarks. The import prices remain exactly where the deck found them: unverified company claims.
