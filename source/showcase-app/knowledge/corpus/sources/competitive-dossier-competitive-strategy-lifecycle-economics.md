## Four rivals keep earning after the sale. DeepGrid stops at the invoice.

A module vendor ships once. Every competitor below holds a channel to the vehicle after start of production — which is where the customer relationship actually lives. OTA is the underrated threat on this list. It converts ADAS from a shipped part into a maintained service: the incumbent's field performance improves after SOP, and the customer relationship stays with the integrator. A merchant module vendor cannot answer that with a better die. If DeepGrid intends to sell a subsystem, it must decide now whether it also sells the means to update it — because that decision is an architecture decision, not a commercial one, and it is cheap today and expensive after the first design-in. · Aptiv · Over-the-air updates · LINC platform + Wind River VxWorks; adds features and improves performance on the deployed fleet without a new design cycle · EV-APT-06 · ZF · SCALAR fleet orchestration · A software platform sold alongside the hardware into the same customer · EV-ZF-07 · drivebuddyAI · Video telematics + predictive risk · The box feeds a platform; the platform feeds the parent's insurance business · EV-DBA-01 · Starkenn · Stark-I subscription · 40% of revenue is recurring subscription, not hardware · EV-STK-06 · DeepGrid Semi · None · A perception subsystem sold to an integrator, with no channel to the vehicle after SOP · NO ROW — this is the gap
