## GATEKEEPER

Does not sell what you sell. Decides who gets to. WHO Aptiv 4/5 Sell TO, not against. The buying decision you must win is its vendor-selection, not an end customer's. ## DIFFERENT JOB

Same industry, different buyer, different budget, different clock. Never in the same bid. WHO drivebuddyAI · Starkenn · bitsensing 1/5 each Ignore for the mandate. Track only as evidence that retrofit demand is real.

## NARRATIVE RIVAL
