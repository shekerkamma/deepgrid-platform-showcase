## Four contests to lose deliberately

The dossier says where to compete. It never says where to stop — so every arena stays notionally open and scarce evidence spend gets spread across all of them. These are arena exits, not pipeline-qualification rules. Each is falsifiable: the trigger column states what would reopen it. · 1 Jan 2027 line-fit · UNWINNABLE ON TIME · Design-in for an OEM programme runs 18–30 months. It is August 2026. A programme binding 1 Jan 2027 was specified before DeepGrid could have entered it. · Reopen only if an OEM offers a mid-cycle perception swap with no re-homologation. · Full-system AEBS supply · STRUCTURALLY CLOSED · Rule 96(12) obliges a system that brakes. The safety case, calibration and warranty sit with the actuator owner, and ZF will not hand those over. · Reopen only if a braking Tier-1 offers a white-label safety case — which none has ever done. · Warning-only retrofit on price · ADVANTAGE IS WORTHLESS HERE · drivebuddyAI, bitsensing and Starkenn already hold it, and it requires no certification at all — so the functional-safety pathway DeepGrid must still ACQUIRE would buy nothing here even once it exists.
