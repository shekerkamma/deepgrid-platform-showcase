All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## The one holding the seat owns no silicon at all

DeepGrid's thesis is owned silicon. Mapping who actually depends on whom tests that thesis directly — and the answer is uncomfortable. Two of the three companies that reach brake actuation buy their perception rather than build it, and the company occupying the exact seat DeepGrid wants owns no silicon whatsoever. Meanwhile the two players with the most credible silicon story — Gahan and Netrasemi — have no route to an Indian CV buyer between them. On this evidence, silicon ownership is neither necessary nor sufficient to hold the perception seat.
