## The same company can be a customer and a rival at once

Brandenburger & Nalebuff's Value Net places four roles around a firm. The dossier collapsed all four into one axis called threat — which is why ZF, a company DeepGrid intends to sell through, was ranked as an adversary. Three companies sit in two quadrants at once. ZF and Aptiv are customers today and become competitors only if they in-source the perception layer — which is precisely the counter-move page's finding, and precisely why the counter-move risk and the blocker rank disagree.
