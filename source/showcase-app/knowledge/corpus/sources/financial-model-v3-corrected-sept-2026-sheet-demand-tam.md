Every line maps to a real, sized market: our FY32 volumes are a single-digit share of each pool. Demand headroom is large, not stretched.
Reading the capture %:
1. Market sizes above are GLOBAL. Our near-term serviceable market is the India slice, where our share is materially higher — the small % = runway, not a ceiling.
2. Seaport AGV pool ($10.95B) is TOTAL port automation (cranes + software + vehicles); the autonomous-vehicle layer we sell is a subset. Real proof point: live order at Vizag Container Terminal.
3. Mandate timing: DDAWS / blind-spot / LDWS suite is live Apr & Oct 2026 (what AD2 demand rests on). The AEBS braking piece (AIS-162/IS-11852) was deferred to Oct 1 2027 (Gadkari, Lok Sabha, Feb 2026) — does not affect the 2026 ADAS suite.
