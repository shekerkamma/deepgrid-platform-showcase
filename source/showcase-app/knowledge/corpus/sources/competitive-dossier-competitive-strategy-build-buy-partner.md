## Buy the certification, partner the homologation, build only the perception

The gate analysis names a functional-safety pathway as the highest-value proof gate. That is a procurement decision before it is an engineering one, and the deck never framed it as one. · ISO 26262 functional-safety pathway · BUY · A certification route is bought from an assessor and a consultancy, on a known timetable. Building the competence in-house delays the only credential that unlocks Tier-2 sales. · STRADVISION already holds it (EV-SV-05). This is the single gate that decides whether an integrator can even consider DeepGrid. · Indian homologation and test capability · PARTNER · ZF has an in-house Indian test track; DeepGrid does not and should not fund one. ARAI and ICAT are the neutral route and their reports carry more weight with a third party anyway. · Independent characterisation is worth more than self-reported kilometres — the gap in ZF's own 300,000 km claim (EV-ZF-03). · mmWave radar silicon · DO NOT BUILD · DeepGrid's position is camera-based perception. Radar is a separate competence with its own qualification path, and TI, NXP and bitsensing already occupy it. · Gahan AI is the cautionary case: 15 people carrying a radar-to-software claim no third party corroborates (EV-GHN-01, EV-GHN-04). · Route to an OEM · PARTNER · Sterling Tools took the local manufacturing, channel and compliance role beside a foreign perception stack.
