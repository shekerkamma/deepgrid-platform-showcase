# DeepGrid Semi — sources/competitive-dossier-pyramid

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## Governing thought

DeepGrid cannot win India CV ADAS as a full-system supplier — ZF and Aptiv own braking, ECU, calibration, approval and warranty. It can win as the bounded perception layer an accountable integrator embeds, and the only thing between here and there is reproducible evidence that four funded gates buy in 90 days.

## The field is occupied by control point, not by product.

A competitor is dangerous when it owns something DeepGrid must pass through. ZF and Aptiv hold the gates that decide a programme; no feature answers that. Act II · four arenas, four control points

## No competitor placement in this dossier is independently verified.

Every rating rests on company statements, not on procurement or homologation artifacts, so the war room must move scores on evidence events rather than announcements. Act III · ten evidence ladders and falsifiers

## DeepGrid’s current claims outrun its evidence, and that is the binding constraint.

The April-2026 mandate date is wrong, five claims are unsupported, and a projected die price is being compared to a system price. Proof deficit — not feature deficit — is what blocks entry. Act IV · the wedge · Act V · the 90-day sprint
