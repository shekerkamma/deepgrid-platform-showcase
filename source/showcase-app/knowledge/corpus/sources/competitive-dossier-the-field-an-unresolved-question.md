## Aptiv's win and STRADVISION's win may be the same programme

The dossier counts these as two competitive events. The evidence does not establish that they are. If it is one programme, the field has one Indian CV line-fit win between Aptiv and STRADVISION, not two, and the dossier overstates the pace of competitive capture. If it is two, then STRADVISION is selling into India independently of Aptiv as well as inside it, which makes it a broader threat than the profile allows. Both readings change a rating. Neither is established, and the honest move is to carry the question rather than pick the flattering answer. UNRESOLVED — do not merge, do not double-count
