## One of these nine is a competitor. The other eight are something else.

Threat rating asks how dangerous a company is. It presumes the company is bidding for the same thing — and by the dossier's own obstruction scores, six of nine are not. Pattern first, rating second. A threat ranking across all nine therefore compares things that were never comparable, and it spends the scarce evidence budget on companies DeepGrid will never bid against.
