## Source bias — stated plainly

The AIS-140 price evidence (EV-09 … EV-13) comes predominantly from fleetx, a fleet telematics vendor publishing buyer guides. Three of four price rows trace to one commercial publisher with an interest in emphasising total cost of ownership over sticker price. That bias runs in the same direction as the finding it supports — a telematics vendor benefits from readers concluding the box is more expensive than it looks. The finding is therefore directionally suspect and is labelled medium confidence, not high, and the deck should attribute it rather than assert it. Corroboration from a non-telematics source (an RTO circular, an operator association, a fleet's own procurement note) is the single highest-value next retrieval.
