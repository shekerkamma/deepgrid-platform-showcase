# DeepGrid Semi — sources/competitive-dossier-hostile-questions

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## “India-native” is our differentiator — why retire it?

It is not a control point. Government buyers reward demonstrable performance and procurement credibility; nationality alone has never cleared a homologation gate. Lead with measured constrained-compute VRU performance instead.

## If ZF and Aptiv own the vehicle, is there any position left at all?

Yes — the perception layer they choose to embed. That is a Tier-2 position inside their safety case, which is exactly why the market-entry event is a named braking Tier-1 accepting our interfaces, not an OEM win.

## Starkenn shows 2,500+ vehicles. Are we not already behind?

The number does not survive its own source. The single Nov-2024 interview it comes from also says ‘over 700 units sold’ and ‘300+ vehicles have deployed our tech’ — three figures for one quantity, self-reported and unaudited, now ~21 months old. Starkenn is a 57-person company shrinking 15.8% year on year on $2.0m of lifetime funding. What is real is its 30% government-tender share and 40% subscription revenue — empanelment and recurring revenue, not scale. Compete on the tender route, not against a deployment count that cannot be quoted. (EV-STK-04, EV-STK-05)

## The ASIC gives us a 3–7× cost advantage. Why gate the capital?

Because a projected die price is not comparable to a system price. The bridge must carry NRE, yield, package, qualification, warranty and lifecycle. Commission the break-even model first; the direction is not the magnitude.

## Why only four gates? Can we not run more in parallel?

Four parallel gates already exceed the engineering and management capacity of a company this size. Running six dilutes all of them rather than clearing any one. The stop rule is explicit: re-plan if two of four miss their first milestone.

## What would make you change this recommendation?

A named Indian CV OEM qualifying DeepGrid directly, with no braking Tier-1 in the loop, before 1 October 2027. That single observable overturns the attach-and-prove thesis, and it is on the page.
