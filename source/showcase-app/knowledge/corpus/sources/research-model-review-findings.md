## Financial strengths (must survive the rebuild)

- Revenue built from unit volume × price per named product — makes the top line testable against capacity, channel and market size. - Selling prices held flat for six years; all growth comes from volume. - FY2027 presented honestly as an investment year at −30% EBITDA margin. - EBITDA and net income both turn positive in FY2028 — a short path to self-funding reduces capital at risk. - Segment definitions stated explicitly, so the revenue split is auditable. - Overheads scale smoothly rather than stepping.

## Not findings

- 197% revenue CAGR. Ambition is the asset at this stage, and the plan decomposes growth into units and prices — the correct test, which it passes. - Flat six-year pricing. Reads as conservative. It matters only in combination with F-03/F-05, because it forces the whole margin burden onto cost. - Rounding drift ≤ ₹0.01 Cr across several P&L identities — inside the plan's own precision.
