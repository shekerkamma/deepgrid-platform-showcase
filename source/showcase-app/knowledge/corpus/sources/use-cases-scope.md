# DeepGrid Semi — sources/use-cases-scope

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## The bounding statement

DeepGrid sells a bounded camera-perception subsystem inside an accountable integrator's safety case. It does not own brake actuation, calibration, homologation or warranty, and no use case below may be written as if it does. Do not fund: A full-stack braking safety case · broad OEM customisation · a radar programme of any kind · any performance marketing not backed by a measured result. Release capital only when partner access, field telemetry and a primary qualification artifact clear together.
