## Autonomous TaaS (product dossier, source slide 42)

Autonomous TaaS (Fleet & Mobility, Services). Transport-as-a-Service: contracted autonomous operations — DeepGrid provides the vehicle, stack and driver replacement as a metered service. Price ₹66.00 L. FY2032 revenue projection ₹66 Cr, 5.8% of the plan, gross margin 89%, first revenue FY2028, volume 100 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: Highest revenue per unit we sell. Proof that the same silicon running in a kit can run an entire operation. From sensing to action: Route plan set by fleet management system; SoC2 handles full perception and planning stack; Vehicle drives autonomously within geofenced route; Fleet dashboard monitors all units in real time. Key dependency: Regulatory clearance for autonomous operation in controlled corridors.
