# DeepGrid Semi — products/ad2

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## Smart Truck (AD2 kit) (product dossier, source slide 18)

Smart Truck (AD2 kit) (Road Autonomy, Systems). Forward camera, radar, compute box, driver display and the harness that talks to the vehicle. The biggest single line in the plan and the one the mandate is written for. Price ₹2,50,000. FY2032 revenue projection ₹450 Cr, 39.9% of the plan, gross margin 89%, first revenue FY2027, volume 18,000 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: Forty percent of group revenue from one SKU. That is the concentration to underwrite — also why the portfolio has a demand floor rather than a forecast. From sensing to action: Camera and radar watch the forward path and the blind quarters; SoC2 fuses both into one object list carrying range and closing speed; Collision, lane and blind-spot logic decides whether to warn; Driver is warned; where vehicle supports it, brake interface is signalled. Key dependency: AIS-mandate compliance, installation channel capacity, and radar verification milestones.
