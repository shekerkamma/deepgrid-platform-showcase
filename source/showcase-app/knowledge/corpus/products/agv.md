# DeepGrid Semi — products/agv

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## Seaport AGV (product dossier, source slide 46)

Seaport AGV (Fleet & Mobility, Systems). Autonomous container yard vehicle — 7cm open positioning, 22cm under crane. Moves containers between quay crane and yard block. Price ₹45,30,000. FY2032 revenue projection ₹22.6 Cr, 2.0% of the plan, gross margin 89%, first revenue FY2028, volume 50 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: Second-highest revenue per unit after TaaS — validates the silicon in port infrastructure. From sensing to action: GPS-RTK and vision provide centimetre-level localisation; SoC2 plans path through dynamic yard traffic; Autonomous steer/throttle/brake in open and under-crane zones; Yard management system dispatches and monitors fleet. Key dependency: Port authority partnerships and certification.
