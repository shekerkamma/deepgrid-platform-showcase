## H100 Driver Monitor (product dossier, source slide 62)

H100 Driver Monitor (Sensors & Robotics, Components). In-cabin driver monitoring unit — drowsiness, distraction and seatbelt detection. Mandate-listed safety function. Price ₹5,000. FY2032 revenue projection ₹10 Cr, 0.9% of the plan, gross margin 50%, first revenue FY2027, volume 20,000 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: Highest volume sensor SKU — mandate requires it in every commercial vehicle. From sensing to action: IR camera watches driver face; SoC2 runs drowsiness/distraction model; Alert escalation chain triggered; Event log for fleet compliance. Key dependency: Mandate enforcement timeline.
