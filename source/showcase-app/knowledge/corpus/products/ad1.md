# DeepGrid Semi — products/ad1

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## AD1 Indoor L4 Kit (product dossier, source slide 12)

AD1 Indoor L4 Kit (Road Autonomy, Systems). Self-driving retrofit kit for indoor vehicles: sensor head, compute box and drive-by-wire interface. Fitted to forklifts and tugs a site already owns. Price ₹1,00,000. FY2032 revenue projection ₹42 Cr, 3.7% of the plan, gross margin 89%, first revenue FY2028, volume 4,200 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: Where L4 behaviour ships years before it is legal on a public road — the technical proof point for the road roadmap. From sensing to action: Cameras and depth sensing read the aisle, load and people; SoC2 localises the vehicle against a site map it holds onboard; Planner picks a path and a speed for the geofenced route; Drive-by-wire interface issues steer, throttle and brake. Key dependency: Site mapping and commissioning throughput — services capacity rather than product.
