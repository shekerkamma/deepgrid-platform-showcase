# DeepGrid Semi — products/radar

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## 4D Radar Pod (product dossier, source slide 64)

4D Radar Pod (Sensors & Robotics, Components). 4D imaging radar module providing range, velocity, azimuth and elevation — the sensor that camera-only ADAS cannot replace. Price ₹25,000. FY2032 revenue projection ₹7.5 Cr, 0.7% of the plan, gross margin 50%, first revenue FY2027, volume 3,000 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: Radar matters because closing speed is what a braking decision actually turns on — camera alone cannot measure it. From sensing to action: 4D radar scans forward scene; Range + velocity + angle data output; SoC2 fuses radar with camera object list; Closing-speed data enables braking decisions. Key dependency: Radar block verification on the ASIC.
