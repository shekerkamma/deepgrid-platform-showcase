# DeepGrid Semi — products/chipset

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## Chipset OEM B2B (product dossier, source slide 26)

Chipset OEM B2B (Silicon & Compute, Components). The bare die, in tray or reel, for a customer to place on their own board. No enclosure, no cables, no fitting — silicon and a datasheet. Price ₹18,000. FY2032 revenue projection ₹54 Cr, 4.8% of the plan, gross margin 94%, first revenue FY2028, volume 30,000 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: Fifty-four crore at ninety-four percent gross margin — the highest in the portfolio. Every rupee arrives without installation capacity or warranty logistics. From sensing to action: Customer's own sensors feed their own board; Our die does the perception compute inside their design; Their software decides what to do with the result; Their product ships with our silicon inside it. Key dependency: ASIC tapeout completion and qualification.
