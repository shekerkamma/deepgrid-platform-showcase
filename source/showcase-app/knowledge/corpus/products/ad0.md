## AD0 Smart Mirror (product dossier, source slide 8)

AD0 Smart Mirror (Road Autonomy, Systems). A replacement rear-view mirror with an integrated display, four body cameras and a wiring loom. Fitted in an afternoon — the least sophisticated thing we make, and it ships first. Price ₹50,000. FY2032 revenue projection ₹270 Cr, 23.9% of the plan, gross margin 89%, first revenue FY2027, volume 54,000 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: Volume ramp of the portfolio: highest unit count and lowest fitment complexity — scales channel reach that harder products inherit. From sensing to action: Four cameras cover the vehicle's blind quarters; SoC2 stitches the views into one 360° surround image; Proximity and motion logic flags anything entering the danger zone; Driver sees the stitched view and hears a directional alert. Key dependency: Distribution reach and price holding at ₹50k. Runs on current-generation hardware — not gated on the ASIC.
