## A100 Compute Box 2ch (product dossier, source slide 32)

A100 Compute Box 2ch (Silicon & Compute, Modules). Dual-channel compute board for stereo vision and dual-camera applications. Price ₹1,60,000. FY2032 revenue projection ₹28.8 Cr, 2.5% of the plan, gross margin 89%, first revenue FY2028, volume 1,800 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: Mid-tier compute module serving stereo vision applications.
