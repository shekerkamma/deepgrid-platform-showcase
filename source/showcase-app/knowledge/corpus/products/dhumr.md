# DeepGrid Semi — products/dhumr

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## Defence D-HUMR (product dossier, source slide 56)

Defence D-HUMR (Sensors & Robotics, Systems). Defence-grade unmanned ground reconnaissance platform. Hardened compute, encrypted comms and ruggedised sensor suite. Price ₹20,00,000. FY2032 revenue projection ₹30 Cr, 2.7% of the plan, gross margin 68%, first revenue FY2027, volume 150 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: Highest price point in the portfolio — opens defence procurement channel and validates ruggedised silicon. From sensing to action: Ruggedised camera and thermal sensors scan terrain; SoC2 runs perception with military-grade encryption; Autonomous navigation in GPS-denied environments; Secure data relay to command post. Key dependency: Defence procurement cycle and security clearances.
