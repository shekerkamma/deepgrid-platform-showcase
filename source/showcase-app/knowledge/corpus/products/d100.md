# DeepGrid Semi — products/d100

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## D100 Drone SoC Kit (product dossier, source slide 58)

D100 Drone SoC Kit (Sensors & Robotics, Modules). Aerial inspection and surveillance compute kit — same SoC2 in a drone-optimised form factor. Price ₹5,00,000. FY2032 revenue projection ₹20 Cr, 1.8% of the plan, gross margin 68%, first revenue FY2028, volume 400 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: Opens aerial perception as a silicon market — same die, different firmware. From sensing to action: Gimbal camera + thermal sensor; SoC2 runs airborne perception; Object detection and tracking; Telemetry relay to ground station. Key dependency: Drone regulation and form-factor development.
