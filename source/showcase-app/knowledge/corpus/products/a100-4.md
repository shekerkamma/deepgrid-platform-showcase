# DeepGrid Semi — products/a100-4

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## A100 Compute Box 4ch PCIe (product dossier, source slide 34)

A100 Compute Box 4ch PCIe (Silicon & Compute, Modules). Four-channel PCIe compute card for multi-camera deployments — industrial inspection, security and infrastructure monitoring. Price ₹3,00,000. FY2032 revenue projection ₹36 Cr, 3.2% of the plan, gross margin 89%, first revenue FY2028, volume 1,200 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: The highest-capacity compute form factor — serves customers who need multi-camera perception without building their own board. From sensing to action: Up to four camera feeds enter via PCIe interface; SoC2 runs parallel perception on all four channels; Object lists and alerts output to host system; Customer software consumes structured perception data. Key dependency: ASIC tapeout and PCIe interface validation.
