# DeepGrid Semi — products/thermal

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## Thermal Camera Pod (product dossier, source slide 60)

Thermal Camera Pod (Sensors & Robotics, Components). LWIR thermal camera sensor module for night and monsoon conditions — the perception layer that cameras alone cannot provide. Price ₹50,000. FY2032 revenue projection ₹16 Cr, 1.4% of the plan, gross margin 50%, first revenue FY2027, volume 3,200 @ FY32. Role in the portfolio: The perception layer that enables night and monsoon operation — differentiator for the Smart Truck kit. From sensing to action: LWIR sensor captures thermal scene; SoC2 fuses thermal with visible camera; Enhanced detection in low/no visibility; Alert output to vehicle safety system. Key dependency: LWIR sensor sourcing and calibration pipeline.
