# DeepGrid Semi — use-cases/UC-06

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## UC-06 Government and PSU perception kit

Government and PSU perception kit. Standard: PPP-MII Order 2017 (rev. 2020) · GFR 153(iii). Dates: Transactable now — does not wait for 2027. Challenge: The shipping product is an FPGA module. Qualified volume silicon lands H1 2028 — after the mandate’s all-models date — and OEM design-in runs 18 to 30 months. Every rupee before H1 2028 has to come from the FPGA module. What Deepgrid supplies: The module packaged as a GeM-listed Class-I local-content perception kit for state transport undertakings and PSU fleets, where the gate is eligibility rather than certification. Steps: Compute and document local content at or above 50%, self-certified with the locations of value addition; List on GeM with country of origin and local-content percentage, qualifying for the Make in India filter; Bid into STU, PSU and municipal tenders reserved for or preference-weighted toward Class-I suppliers; Instrument every delivered unit and secure telemetry rights in the supply contract — these units are the evidence engine for UC-02. Buyer: STU procurement officer · depot engineer · Deepgrid BD · Head of Validation Gate: A live GeM listing with documented local content at or above 50%, and one Class-I tender bid submitted with telemetry rights in the draft contract. Risk: An incumbent already draws about 30% of revenue from government tenders and can underbid on empanelment. Class-I status is winnable but slowly, and a nodal ministry may prescribe local content above 50%.
