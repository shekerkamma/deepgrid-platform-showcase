## UC-05 Lane departure warning

Lane departure warning. Standard: AIS-188 (LDWS) · CMVR 98(6). Dates: 1 Oct 2027 new models · 1 Jan 2028 all models. Challenge: 58.6% of 2024 road deaths were on national and state highways — about 5% of the network — and 67.6% of accidents happened on straight roads, precisely where LDWS applies. Yet markings on those corridors are worn, inconsistent or absent. What Deepgrid supplies: Lane estimation that does not depend on a painted line: road edge, kerb, median, worn marking and vehicle-flow cues fused into a drivable-corridor estimate, with thresholds that move with traffic density and speed. Steps: Segment lane marking, road edge, kerb and median, and estimate marking quality; Estimate the drivable corridor and lateral position when no marking is visible; Modulate the departure threshold by traffic density and speed; suppress during indicated manoeuvres; Emit the departure event with confidence, plus an explicit temporarily-unavailable state. Buyer: OEM chassis and ADAS engineer · Tier-1 integrator · long-haul driver Gate: A measured corridor-estimation and alert result on unmarked and degraded Indian highway sections, reporting availability rate alongside false-alert rate. Risk: Declaring the function unavailable too readily is the easy way to pass a nuisance target and the fast way to fail type approval. Both numbers must be reported together or neither means anything.
