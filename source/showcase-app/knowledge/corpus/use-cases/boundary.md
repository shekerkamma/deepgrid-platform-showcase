# DeepGrid Semi — use-cases/boundary

All figures are management projections from DeepGrid's fundraising materials.

## What Deepgrid sells, and what it does not

Deepgrid sells a bounded camera-perception subsystem inside an accountable integrator’s safety case. It does not own brake actuation, calibration, homologation or warranty — and no use case may be written as if it does. Capital is not for: A full-stack braking safety case · broad OEM customisation · a radar programme of any kind · any performance marketing not backed by a measured result. Release capital only when partner access, field telemetry and a primary qualification artifact clear together.. Sequence: Now – 30 days: GeM listing prepared · nuisance taxonomy and protocol drafted with ARAI or ICAT · ISO 26262 assessor engaged as a procurement action (CEO · CTO); 30 – 60 days: Technical exchange opened with a braking Tier-1 · three paid pilots contracted with telemetry rights · drowsiness study cohort assembled (CEO · Validation); 60 – 90 days: First stratified nuisance result published · first Class-I bid submitted · written go/no-go recorded from the Tier-1 exchange (Validation · BD).
