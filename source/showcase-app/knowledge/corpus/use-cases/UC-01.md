## UC-01 AEBS forward-path perception

AEBS forward-path perception. Standard: AIS-162 / IS 11852:2019 · CMVR 96(12). Dates: 1 Jan 2027 new models · 1 Oct 2027 all models. Challenge: Rule 96(12) obliges AEBS plus Vehicle Stability Function on M2/M3/N2/N3. The system must warn at least 1.4 s before it brakes and may not enter the emergency phase before TTC 3.0 s. That budget is a perception problem; the braking is not. What Deepgrid supplies: Detection, classification, tracking and per-target TTC on a documented interface, with confidence and sensor-health state. Actuation, calibration, homologation and warranty stay with the Tier-1. Steps: Forward camera into the module: classify car, truck, bus, two- and three-wheeler, pedestrian and animal from 25 km/h to max design speed, at every load state; Track each target, estimate range-rate, compute per-target TTC and in-path probability; Emit a ranked object list with per-object confidence and a degradation state on the interface the Tier-1 specifies; The Tier-1 arbitrates the warning cascade and owns the braking decision and its safety case. Buyer: Tier-1 ADAS system engineer · functional-safety manager · OEM chief engineer Gate: A braking Tier-1 accepts the interface, diagnostics, safety assumptions and validation plan in writing, with a recorded go/no-go. Risk: No published ISO 26262 pathway. Until an assessor is engaged, the integrator is comparing a certified incumbent with an uncertified newcomer, and no perception result closes that gap.
