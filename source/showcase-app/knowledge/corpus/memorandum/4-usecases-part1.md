Six use cases carry the commercial plan. Five are regulated functions a truck maker must source; the sixth is the only arena that can transact before qualified silicon exists. The bounding statement Deepgrid sells a bounded camera-perception subsystem inside an accountable integrator’s safety case. It does not own brake actuation, calibration, homologation or warranty — and no use case may be written as if it does. That sentence is the most investor-relevant line in the whole file. It is what makes the Tier-1 sale possible, it caps the liability the company carries, and abandoning it is what would make the sale impossible.
