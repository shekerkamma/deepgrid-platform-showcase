# Exa search findings — 10 Aug 2026

Ran after You.com livecrawl found no placeable imagery. Exa surfaced named press coverage
(Business Standard, New Indian Express, ET Manufacturing, TechGig, Communications Today,
T-Works and My Startup TV on LinkedIn), March–June 2026.

**It found little usable imagery — and three facts that contradict the deck's core numbers.**

---

## 1. The BOM figures in the deck do not match what the founder says publicly

This affects the deck's single most-repeated number, on slides 1, 3, 4, 11, 12, 29, 41 and 55.

| | Deck says | Founder says publicly (Mar 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| FPGA compute cost today | **Rs 65,000** | **Rs 35,000** per chipset |
| Post-tapeout target | **Rs 1,750** | **Rs 3,000** per chipset (≈$30 at ~1M units) |
| Saving per unit | **Rs 63,250** | **≈Rs 32,000** |

Sources: Business Standard 22 Mar 2026; ET Manufacturing 24 Mar 2026; TechGig 23 Mar 2026;
Communications Today 23 Mar 2026 — all quoting Aravind Prasad directly:
*"We have achieved the technology know-how and started deploying it in an FPGA, where the cost
for one chipset is around ₹35,000. If we want to bring the cost down to ₹3,000 per chipset, we
must tape out, which will cost around $3 million."*

New Indian Express (2 Apr 2026) gives the scale figure differently again: *"cost from about
$3000 to $30 at scale… around a million units."*

**Consequence:** the headline saving is roughly **half** what the deck claims, and the deck's
`Rs 65,000 → Rs 1,750` waterfall — drawn to scale on slide 11 — is not reconcilable with the
founder's public statements. Resolve which is correct before this deck is shown to anyone who
can read a newspaper.

## 2. The tapeout number is no longer an estimate — it is public

The council flagged `Rs 20–35 Cr` as a planning-grade figure requiring validation. It is now
corroborated: **₹25 crore raise, explicitly for tapeout and marketing** (~$3 million).

This *strengthens* the council's central arithmetic rather than weakening it. Rs 25 Cr ÷
Rs 1.125 lakh gross per kit ≈ 2,200 kits — computed against a figure the founder has stated
on the record. The funding gap is real and publicly documented.

Timing: tapeout targeted "within the next 10 months" from March 2026 → roughly **January 2027**,
*contingent on the raise closing*. Earlier than the deck's Q1–mid 2027, and conditional.

## 3. A third product name, and prior silicon that the deck does not mention

- **New Indian Express** calls the part **DGrid-SoC**. The deck calls it **DGS001**. The website
  calls it **DG-A100**. Three names for what appears to be one product line.
- **A 130nm tape-out for silicon validation has already been done**, at ~Rs 20 lakh. The deck
  treats the company as entirely pre-silicon. Prior validated silicon is a materially stronger
  position than the deck claims.
- Company **founded 2024**, based at **T-Hub Phase 2, Hyderabad**. Founder and CEO:
  **Aravind Prasad Govardhan**.
