# Story Pack — DeepGrid Semi, Segmented Model ADAS v3

Produced per `story-architect`. Feeds the branded PPTX build.

## 1. BLUF

The segmentation in v3 is presentationally strong but not yet evidentially
bottom-up, and three fixable defects — one arithmetic, one disclosure, one
margin-mechanism — will surface in the first serious diligence pass. Fix them
before the model circulates further; none require changing the plan's ambition.

## 2. Audience decision

**Audience:** founder / CEO preparing a ₹55 Cr raise.
**Decision:** what to correct, rebuild, and prepare a defense for, before the
model goes to another investor.
Not a verdict on whether the business is fundable — a readiness verdict on the
document.

## 3. Tension

The model's own Reconciliation tab already documents that the June 2026
Information Memorandum, now in investors' hands, disagrees with it. Every week
the current model circulates unreconciled, the number of counterparties holding
two contradictory documents grows. Separately, the plan's nearest-term promise —
EBITDA breakeven in FY2028 — rests on a 20-point gross-margin jump in a single
year, and has only a 6.3-point cushion. That is the first thing an analyst
stress-tests, and it is the claim closest to the money.

## 4. Argument arc

1. **Context** — v3 exists to segment a plan the company already believes in;
   company-level figures are unchanged by design.
2. **Credit** — the build is genuinely decomposed to units × ASP per product,
   which is better than most material at this stage.
3. **Tension** — but three defects are visible to any analyst in under an hour.
4. **Proof A (arithmetic)** — FY2028 net income contradicts the model's own
   EBT and tax lines, and understates the company's story.
5. **Proof B (disclosure)** — the IM and the model disagree in opposite
   directions on linked figures, so at least one was never re-derived.
6. **Proof C (mechanism)** — segment margins are identical to one decimal across
   three unrelated businesses; the mix defense is arithmetically unavailable
   (6% of the expansion), and the residual converts to a ₹27,500 kit BOM.
7. **Implication** — the fragile claim is near-term, not terminal: FY2028
   breakeven, not FY2032 scale.
8. **Action** — a ranked fix list, sequenced by what each defect costs in
   diligence.

## 6. Evidence map

**Direct evidence (Verified — re-derived from the file):**
FY2028 net income break (₹2.29 Cr); segment totals vs P&L drift in FY30/FY31;
identical per-segment margins; margin bridge 6%/94%; breakeven-GM cushions;
FY2032 concentration; R&D-to-payroll ratio; absence of formulas, cash flow,
uses of funds.

**Fair synthesis:** the IM/model contradiction is documented in the workbook's
own Reconciliation tab; characterizing it as disclosure exposure is synthesis.
The ₹27,500 BOM is arithmetic; calling it implausible for compute + software +
sensors is synthesis.

**Interpretation (Judgement — no external benchmark run):** that 89% is
unreachable for a bundled hardware kit; implied headcount at ₹25L loaded cost;
that flat six-year ASPs understate price erosion. All are analyst opinion and
must be labelled as such on the slide, not presented as benchmarked.

## 7. Content cuts

- Rounding drift within ±0.02 — noise; annex only.
- The 197% CAGR as a criticism — it is the asset being sold, not a defect.
- Full extract dump — annex, not slides.
- Any implied valuation view — outside the requested scope.

- Slide 19 is mandatory; a findings-only deck gets discounted wholesale.
- Speaker notes carry the narrative description for every slide.
