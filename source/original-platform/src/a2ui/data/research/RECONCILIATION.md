## 4. Retired claims still live in the prior deck

The prior deck is not clean. It asserts, on slide 31:

> **"12.9× COST ADVANTAGE vs. Mobileye $50 ASP (sourced)"**

This is on the retired list — it compares their ASP to our die cost. It must not be carried
forward into any new deck.

It also states **"₹1 Cr already received"** with its own red-team flag that the nature of the
revenue is unconfirmed. Later work resolved this: **₹23.01 lakh delivered**, ₹78.39 lakh is L1
(lowest bidder, not an award), sitting in **Deepgrid Datacentre** — a different legal entity —
with customer-owned IP, and none of it trucking or ADAS revenue. The prior deck's figure is
~4× the real one.

**My playbook's slide 14 ("What we are not claiming") is the only artifact in the set that
gets this right.** It should be carried into any merged deck unchanged.

## 5. What the prior deck does better than mine

1. **A weighted scorecard with published weights.** Mine asserts 50/30/20 with no derivation —
   exactly the gap the council's Stranger flagged. Theirs shows its work.
2. **Kill criteria per segment.** "At least 1 of the first 20 buyer interviews must convert to a
   paid pilot within 90 days, or this segment stays secondary."
3. **A red team and a conditional decision.** Stage 5 returns *Proceed With Conditions* with five
   named, ordered gates — including "confirm the exact nature of the ₹1 Cr revenue" as a
   one-week task gating everything else.
4. **An anti-ICP slide.** Segments deliberately excluded, named.

## 6. Recommended resolution

1. **Adopt the prior scorecard's ranking.** Re-weight to roughly **50% Tactical Defense &
   Off-Highway/Port · 35% Fleet Retrofit · 15% OEM/Tier-1 relationship-building only.**
   Do not resource OEM at 30%.
2. **Keep my Decision 01, 04, the claim-hygiene slide, the gazette evidence and the
   qualification scorecard** — these have no counterpart in the prior work.
3. **Take their phase numbering, kill criteria, anti-ICP slide and red-team conditions.**
4. **Freeze all BOM figures** until §3 is resolved. Three sources, three answers.
5. **Strike "12.9×" and "₹1 Cr defence revenue"** wherever they appear.
