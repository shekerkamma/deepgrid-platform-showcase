NEW YORK - Roadzen Inc. (NASDAQ:RDZN) announced today that its drivebuddyAI platform secured a contract to deploy six-camera advanced driver assistance systems across 3,000 heavy-duty trucks operated by an Indian fleet company, according to a press release statement.

The deployment will generate approximately $2.5 million in initial contracted revenue. The agreement includes potential expansion to 10,000 vehicles over five years, which would represent approximately $10 million in total contracted value if fully realized.The contract comes as Roadzen, with a market capitalization of $117 million, continues to expand its presence in India’s commercial vehicle safety market. The company’s stock has surged nearly 70% over the past year and gained over 20% in the past week, reflecting growing investor interest in its AI-driven safety solutions.

The customer is an existing drivebuddyAI operator that chose to expand its use of the platform. The fleet operator has doubled annually in recent years and is projected to reach 10,000 vehicles within five years, according to the company.

The system includes driver drowsiness detection, facial recognition for driver identification, cabin occupancy monitoring, driver safety scoring, and cloud telephony for command center operations.

Rohan Malhotra, Founder and CEO of Roadzen, stated that fleet operators are adopting ADAS technology ahead of India’s AIS 184 mandate, which will require new commercial vehicles to include ADAS at the manufacturer level.

Nisarg Pandya, Founder and CEO of drivebuddyAI, said the platform combines real-time driver monitoring, collision prevention, video telematics, and predictive safety intelligence. The company stated the platform has been trained on over 4 billion kilometers of driving data and has demonstrated a greater than 70% reduction in on-road risk events across deployed fleets.Despite the growth momentum, [InvestingPro](https://www.investing.com/pro/RDZN) analysis indicates the company is quickly burning through cash while revenue reached $50.3 million in the last twelve months with 17% growth. Analysts do not anticipate profitability this year. Roadzen said drivebuddyAI is the only platform in India to have achieved AIS 184 compliance for new commercial vehicles. The company reported seeing increased demand from fleet operators in hazmat, chemicals, steel, and heavy industrial sectors.

Roadzen is headquartered in Burlingame, California, and employs more than 300 people across offices in the U.S., U.K., and India.

In other recent news, Roadzen Inc. announced that its India-based subsidiary, VehicleCare, secured a significant contract with a major Indian insurer, anticipated to generate over $10 million in annual revenue. This contract will involve processing auto insurance claims through VehicleCare’s extensive repair network. Additionally, Roadzen’s UK subsidiary, Global Insurance Management Limited, has closed several commercial contracts expected to bring in approximately $2.5 million over the next year. These contracts involve partners in the automotive finance, dealer, and fleet sectors both in the UK and internationally.

Roadzen also revealed a new partnership with the insurance services division of a top-10 global automaker in the UK to offer Guaranteed Asset Protection (GAP) insurance to retail customers. This marks Roadzen’s second major collaboration with a Tier-1 global automaker in Europe. In technological advancements, Roadzen’s drivebuddyAI platform has been awarded a patent in India for its AI-powered road hazard detection system, which utilizes satellite data and accelerometer technology for real-time road anomaly detection.

Furthermore, Roadzen conducted its Annual General Meeting of Shareholders, where the election of directors and ratification of the company’s independent auditor were confirmed. At the meeting, 54,457,674 ordinary shares were represented, accounting for approximately 68.36% of the shares entitled to vote.
