03The roadmap math

Flat to cube — and why the cube starves alone

SoC1.2 ships an 8×8 matrix unit on FPGA. SoC2 ships an 8×8×8 cube on 28 nm ASIC. The cube is 8× the compute — but only if memory keeps up. Three coupled decisions.
