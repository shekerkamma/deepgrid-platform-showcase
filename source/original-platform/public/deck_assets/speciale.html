05b · The 3D cube — our compute differentiator

Flat 8×8 → 8×8×8 cube. K becomes hardware depth.

FLATSoC1.2 · FPGA
8×8 matrix · 64 MAC/cycle. The K=8 reduction is iterated in software — 8 cycles to consume one tile. Proves the geometry on hardware we own.
CUBESoC2 · 28nm
8×8×8 systolic · 512 MAC/cycle. K=8 embedded as a pipelined DSP cascade — one K-slice retires every cycle, 8× flat throughput.
SCALESoC2 die
64 cubes × 512 = 32,768 MACs @ 600 MHz → 39.3 TOPS. The sweeping plane is one K-slice resolving per cycle across the depth.
The honest catch we engineered around: a cube does 512 MAC/cycle only if fed 512 B/cycle. 64 cubes on SoC1.x's single global DMA would starve at ~12% utilisation. So each cube gets its own LDMA against an 8-bank L2 (~83% target util). Cube + per-PCORE DMA doesn't fit on FPGA — which is precisely why SoC2 must be 28nm.

We're not asking you to fund SoC4-A today. We surface it because it turns a profitable ADAS-silicon business into a category-defining one — and it's the bet that makes a strategic acquirer move early.

1,024tiles · 32×32 mesh
Each tile = 5-port router + safety wrap + NI. XY dimension-order routing, 2 virtual channels (REQ/RSP), credit flow control — verified RTL with a deadlock-freedom proof.
4PFLOPS · FP4 peak

Per-hop header SECDED (routes on corrected coords), lockstep + TMR supervisor, fault→SAFE. FMEDA: SPFM 99.5%, measured by fault injection.
SoC2 is the business; SoC4-A is the optionality. A sovereign-tech fund knows the value of edge-AI silicon is in the platform trajectory, not one SKU.
