What this animation proves — and where the limits are

The good case (no hot bank). Cubes spread across 8 banks, average ~83% utilization, ~28 effective TOPS. This is the design target.

The pathological case (toggle "hot bank" button). All 56 LDMAs hit bank 0. Now 7 cubes contend per bank, utilization collapses to ~22%. Bank conflicts visible in the orange meter. This is the failure mode firmware must avoid via address hashing.

The real workload sits in between. Realistic SoC2 utilization is 60-70% sustained on YOLO-class workloads — better than starved (12%), worse than ideal (83%). Srikar’s testbench data will pin this exactly.
